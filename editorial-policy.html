Editorial Policy

What we publish

We publish evergreen explainers: guides to concepts that remain useful over time. We do not publish stock tips, price predictions or sponsored recommendations.

How we write

  • Concepts are explained in plain language before any technical term is introduced.
  • Examples use round, hypothetical numbers and are labeled as illustrations.
  • Figures and rules are checked against primary sources such as central banks, statistics agencies and financial regulators.
  • AI tools may assist with research and drafting; every guide is reviewed by our editorial team before publication.

Independence

Advertising on this site is served separately from our editorial content. Advertisers have no influence over what we write.

Corrections

If you find an error, write to hello@fincraftscope.com. We review every report and update guides promptly when a correction is needed.
